# Fretlane privacy policy

The privacy policy now lives on the wiki: **<https://github.com/mauritsE/Fretlane/wiki/Privacy-Policy>**

Its source is [`wiki/Privacy-Policy.md`](../wiki/Privacy-Policy.md) in this repository.

In short: Fretlane does not collect, store or share any personal data. Your library stays on your computer. YouTube and Spotify are contacted only for songs you link to them, through their official embedded players.
