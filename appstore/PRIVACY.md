# Fretlane privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

Fretlane does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **Your library stays on your computer.** Songs, tabs, favorites, the archive and sync points are saved in the app's own folder on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.
- **No account, no analytics, no ads.** Fretlane contains no tracking or advertising code and does not contact any server run by the developer.
- **YouTube videos.** If you link a YouTube video to a song, the video plays in YouTube's embedded player, loaded from youtube.com. YouTube's use of data is covered by [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Songs without a video never contact YouTube.
- **Tabs from a link.** If you import a tab from a web address, Fretlane downloads that file from the address you entered, and nothing else.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/mauritsE/Songstarr/issues.
