# Getting started

## Play a song

Fretlane comes with 22 well-known public-domain songs (Greensleeves, Für Elise, House of the Rising Sun, Canon in D and more), arranged for melody guitar, rhythm guitar, bass and drums, and marked beginner or intermediate.

1. Click a song in the library to open it.
2. Press **Play** (or **Space**). The built-in synthesizer plays the song and the cursor follows the tab.
3. Practise with:
   - **Speed** from 25% to 150%.
   - **Looping:** drag across the tab to select some bars, then press **L**.
   - **Tracks:** switch between guitar, bass and drums, or mute and solo them.
   - **Views:** Tab, Score + Tab or Score; page or horizontal layout; zoom.
   - **Count-in** and a **click track** that follows the tab.

## Metronome

Press **M** (or click **♩ Metronome**). You get tap tempo, accents, beats per bar, subdivisions (eighths, triplets, sixteenths), volume and beat lights. One click sets it to the open song's tempo at your current practice speed. It keeps running while you switch songs.

## Organise your library

- **☆** makes a song a favorite. Favorites are listed first and have their own view.
- **✓ Mark as done** moves a song you've learned to the **Archive**. **↺** brings it back.
- Search by title or artist, and filter by tag.

## Add your own songs

Click **+ Add song** and choose one of:

- **Upload file:** Guitar Pro 3–8 (`.gp3`, `.gp4`, `.gp5`, `.gpx`, `.gp`), MusicXML (`.xml`, `.musicxml`, `.mxl`), [alphaTex](https://alphatab.net/docs/alphatex/introduction) or a plain-text tab.
- **From URL:** a link to a tab file. Fretlane downloads it for you.
- **Paste text:** a plain-text ("ASCII") tab such as `e|---0---3---|`.

Only use tabs you wrote, bought, or are allowed to use. See [Copyright and tabs](Copyright-and-Tabs).

**About plain-text tabs:** text tabs don't contain rhythm, so every note column becomes an eighth note. The notes and bars are right, but the timing inside a bar is approximate. Pin every bar (see below) to keep the cursor on the right bar.

## Link a recording

In the song dialog, paste a **YouTube** link or a **Spotify** track link into the Recording field. Or click **🔍 Find** (or type words and press Enter) to search for the artist and title. Studio recordings are listed first; covers, lessons and live versions sink unless you searched for them, because their timing differs from the record.

- **YouTube** works without an account.
- **Spotify search** needs a free Spotify developer app once; the dialog walks you through it. Playing a Spotify track needs you to be logged in to Spotify, otherwise Spotify plays a 30-second preview. Spotify can't change speed, so Spotify songs always play at 100%. To slow a song down, use a YouTube video of it.
- A song has either a YouTube video or a Spotify track, not both.

## Sync the tab to the recording

1. Open the song and click **⇆ Sync**.
2. Pause the video exactly on the first beat of bar 1 and click **📌 Pin to video time**. For most studio recordings that's all you need.
3. If the band speeds up or slows down, pin more bars the same way. Or click **Tap along**, play the video, and press **T** on the first beat of each bar (**Esc** stops).
4. Use **Shift all** (±0.05 s / ±0.5 s) to nudge everything earlier or later.

Pins are saved automatically.

## Keyboard shortcuts

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| **Space** | Play / pause |
| **L** | Loop the selected bars |
| **M** | Show or hide the metronome |
| **T** | Tap tempo (metronome) or tap a bar (Tap along) |
| **Esc** | Stop Tap along |
