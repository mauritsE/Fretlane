# Copyright and tabs

## What Fretlane ships with

Fretlane bundles no third-party tabs and does not scrape or download tabs from any tab site by itself.

- **Demo tabs** are original works written for Fretlane.
- **Songbook songs** are public-domain melodies (traditional tunes, or composers who died long ago) in Fretlane's own arrangements. The arrangements are part of the open-source project and can be found in [`songbook/songs.ts`](https://github.com/mauritsE/Fretlane/blob/main/songbook/songs.ts).
- **Recordings** are never bundled or downloaded. Linked YouTube videos and Spotify tracks play through those services' official embedded players, so the rights holders keep control over them.

## Tabs you add

You decide what goes into your library, and you're responsible for it. Good sources are:

- tabs you wrote yourself (Guitar Pro, TuxGuitar and MuseScore can all export files Fretlane opens),
- tabs you bought,
- tabs whose license allows you to use them.

Your library is stored only on your computer and is never uploaded or shared by Fretlane.

## Reporting a copyright problem

If you believe something in the Fretlane repository, its releases or this wiki infringes your copyright, open an issue at <https://github.com/mauritsE/Fretlane/issues> with:

1. what the work is and that you own it (or act for the owner),
2. where exactly the material is (file path or link),
3. how to reach you.

It will be looked at promptly and removed if the claim holds. You can also use GitHub's own [DMCA takedown process](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/dmca-takedown-policy).

Fretlane can't remove content from YouTube, Spotify or other websites. Report that to the service that hosts it.
