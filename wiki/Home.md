# Fretlane

Fretlane is a free, open-source tab player for guitar, bass and drums. Open a tab, link a YouTube video or a Spotify track, and play along while the cursor follows the recording. Everything runs on your own computer: there is no account, no tracking and no ads.

- **[Getting started](Getting-Started):** play a song, add your own tabs, and sync a tab to a video.
