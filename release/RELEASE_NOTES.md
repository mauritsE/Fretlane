## Download Fretlane

No installation or technical knowledge needed. Download the file for your computer, unzip it, and open **START HERE.txt**.

| Your computer | Download |
| --- | --- |
| **Windows** 10 / 11 | `Fretlane-…-windows-x64.zip` |
| **Mac** with Apple chip (M1, M2, M3, M4…) | `Fretlane-…-macos-arm64.zip` |
| **Mac** with Intel chip (older Macs) | `Fretlane-…-macos-x64.zip` |
| **Linux** (most PCs) | `Fretlane-…-linux-x64.zip` |
| **Linux** on ARM (e.g. Raspberry Pi 4/5, 64-bit) | `Fretlane-…-linux-arm64.zip` |

Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu → **About This Mac**. "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple chip. "Processor: Intel" means Intel.

### Quick start
1. Unzip the download.
2. Double-click **Fretlane** (on Windows: **Fretlane.exe**).
3. Your browser opens Fretlane. Keep the small Fretlane window open while you play, and close it to quit.

The first time, Windows or macOS will warn you that the app is from an unidentified developer. Fretlane is a free hobby project and isn't registered with Microsoft or Apple. **START HERE.txt** explains how to open it anyway (it takes about 20 seconds, once).

Your songs are saved in a **Fretlane** folder in your home folder, so updating to a newer version keeps your library.
